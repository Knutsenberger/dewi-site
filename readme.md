# DEWI Bündnis

**Deutsche Ethische Wirtschaftliche Ingenieure Bündnis**

An independent analytical initiative focused on decision quality, systems thinking and responsible governance.

## Mission

DEWI aims to contribute to the improvement of public decision-making processes through rational analysis, transparent reasoning and long-term thinking.

The initiative focuses on methods, evaluation criteria and systemic perspectives rather than political campaigning or ideological positions.

## Core Principles

* Evidence-based reasoning
* Responsibility for outcomes
* Systems thinking
* Transparency and traceability
* Long-term societal resilience
* Respect for freedom, rule of law and institutional stability

## What DEWI Is Not

* Not a political party
* Not a protest movement
* Not an ideological platform
* Not a lobbying organization
* Not a replacement for democratic institutions

## Current Status

DEWI is currently in an early development phase.

The project is focused on:

* building a knowledge base
* developing analytical frameworks
* publishing discussion papers and articles
* establishing a community of contributors
